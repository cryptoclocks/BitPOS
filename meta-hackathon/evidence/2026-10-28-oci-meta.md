## 7. First owner-approved promotion and a money-units fix
- At 12:46 ICT, after the owner signed in to the POS and asked for it, I saved a product offer in the POS: Latte 20% off, base 1000 → 800, expires 2026-11-04 23:59 ICT. In the database this is `versioned_product_offers` revision 1, and the audit log has `product.offer.enable`.
- Purple Cafe prices in USD with **4 decimals** (1000 = $0.10). Checkout passed the menu minor units straight to Stripe, which counts in cents, so Latte would have been charged $8.00 instead of $0.08.
  - Fix: `toStripeMinor` converts exactly to Stripe hundredths and refuses fractions of a cent (no rounding). `meta_tap_orders.amount_minor` now stores Stripe units.
  - Prices below Stripe's minimum charge (USD $0.50, THB ฿10) are refused with `BELOW_MINIMUM` and the message "please pay at the counter".
  - Tests: meta 40/40 including the conversion; `tsc` passes. Deployed to the `bitpos-meta` container.
- Consequence: the Latte offer at $0.08 can be shown, but it cannot be paid through Stripe. A Stripe payment demo needs a menu price whose offer price is at least $0.50, and that is the owner's pricing decision.

## 8. Stripe-payable demo price and a same-origin fix
- 12:5x ICT: the owner asked for Latte at USD 5.00 with the 20% offer. I signed in with the POS "Owner" quick sign-in at the owner's request, without typing any password.
  - Saved the menu price: Latte 50000 at 4 decimals. The test catalog name still says "USD 0.10 per item".
  - Re-saved the offer on the new price version: 50000 → 40000, expires 2026-11-04 23:59 ICT. The old offer stays bound to the old price version.
- Tap at 12:52 ICT (counter 1): 200 "welcome back". Muse Spark chose the Latte offer (`chosen_by=model`), reason "Smooth and cozy pick for your midday break".
- Paying returned 403 "Same-origin request required". Cause: the pages set `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, and with that policy browsers send `Origin: null` on same-origin form POSTs.
  - Fix: pages now use `same-origin`, so no referrer is sent cross-origin. `Origin: null` is still refused.
  - Tests: HTTP test added; meta 40/40, `tsc` passes. Redeployed; the public page sends `referrer-policy: same-origin`.
