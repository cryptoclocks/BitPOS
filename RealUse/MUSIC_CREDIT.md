# Background music

The four YouTube field-trial edits use the original instrumental demo bed composed for the BitPOS video production: a synthesized 96 BPM lo-fi arrangement of electric piano, bass, drums, pads and bells, with no external samples. It is mixed quietly at 20% amplitude with entry/exit fades. Original location audio is omitted.

Recordings and photographs remain the builder’s media; the repository’s Apache-2.0 source-code license does not automatically grant media rights.
