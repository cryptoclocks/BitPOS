# Cafe loop video sources

The loop videos in `apps/web/public/videos/menu/` are cut by `tools/runtime/make-product-loops.py` from free stock clips on Pexels
([Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use, attribution not required) plus the menu photo
(see `CAFE_PHOTO_CREDITS.md`). The source clips are not stored in the repository; the table lists the page of each one.
These are real stock footage of the food or drink type, not footage of the demo cafe's own products.
