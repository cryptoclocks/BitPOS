# Crypto World's Fair submission checklist

Prepared 10 October 2026 (Asia/Bangkok). This is a preparation checklist, not a submitted entry or legal eligibility determination.

Official sources: [event](https://colosseum.com/worldsfair), [rules](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf), [FAQ](https://colosseum.com/hackathon).

The event-specific rules state the deadline is **12 October 2026, 11:59 pm Pacific**, corresponding to **13 October 2026, 1:59 pm Thailand**. Confirm the dashboard for changes before submitting.

## Submitter actions still required

- [ ] Register every actual human teammate; confirm eligibility and one-team/one-product rule.
- [ ] Complete the actual dashboard submission form; a GitHub push is not submission.
- [ ] Supply an English **2–3 minute presentation video** and an English **product demo video no longer than 3 minutes**, as described in the FAQ. Confirm exact portal fields.
- [ ] Add final video URLs to README/form. No video URL is fabricated in this repository.
- [ ] Confirm team backgrounds, location, funding history, founder commitment and any real demand/revenue. Do not substitute proposed business plans for traction.
- [ ] Disclose all relevant pre-existing work in the form, using `SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md`.
- [ ] Confirm rights to inherited code/artwork and video/music/person appearances. Decide the license for project-owned source; a public repository alone is not open-source licensing.
- [ ] Ensure the submitted deck/video/description are English and match the actual website/provider checkout. Historical Thai team notes are not the English submission packet; the rules' English-content requirement has not been certified for the entire bilingual repository.
- [ ] If showing Android Phantom approval as proven functionality, complete the deferred real-phone check first.

AI assistance is disclosed in the source history. The FAQ explicitly discusses founders building MVPs with AI tools; that does not exempt submissions from rights, truthful history or other rules.
