# Mint handoff
Five artwork files and local metadata drafts are prepared. No mint performed, no rarity or randomness claims. Relative image paths are local preview references. Before minting, upload images and metadata to chosen durable storage and replace image/file URIs with verified immutable URLs. Add verified collection, creator identity and any required issuer fields through the chosen mint implementation.

Voucher entitlements belong to a server-side issuance and redemption record. Never treat the image, displayed reward name or local metadata as a redeemable credential. No usable coupon codes or fabricated QR codes are included.
