From order to physical confirmation

  1. Create an itemized counter order or order from a table QR on a phone.
  2. Review the bill on the order website and authorize the merchant-sponsored sandbox USDG transaction.
  3. The backend verifies finalized payment, commits inventory and delivers the event to the assigned terminal.
  4. The terminal displays the paid receipt and acknowledges the render.

Counter/table routing and three finalized sponsored payments were verified with physical render ACKs of 404 / 344 / 329 ms. These measurements exclude wallet approval and chain finalization; they are not p95 statistics.

Try it safely

Owner, Manager and Staff quick access is available on the merchant website. Use synthetic contact details only. The environment is shared; please avoid changing device credentials, receiving wallets or other reviewers’ settings.

Payment uses devnet sandbox USDG, not redeemable funds. Signing requires a funded devnet wallet; private keys are not published. Hardware can be reviewed through native screen captures and transaction evidence in the repository.

Current scope

Implemented: merchant menu/cart, table QR ordering, role authorization, one-device register pairing, sponsored payment verification, inventory, durable device delivery and standalone ESP32-S3 firmware.

Pending: physical Android Phantom acceptance, native Actions/Blinks integration, autonomous personalized AI offers and loyalty/NFT rewards. Current checkout uses the order website and wallet provider. Mainnet is disabled.

Development history and evidence

BitPOS builds on existing CryptoClock board/display/touch foundations and earlier Cashless Thailand POS references. The new standalone checkout, reconciliation and device routing are disclosed separately from inherited foundations. AI-assisted coding/design tools were used.
