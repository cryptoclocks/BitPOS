## Cross-cutting facts (referenced by frame rows)

- X1. The three customer pages are `components/Payment.tsx` (route `pages/pay/[accessToken].astro`), `components/PayMenu.tsx` (`pages/pay/[accessToken]/menu.astro`) and `components/TableMenu.tsx` (`pages/table/[entry].astro`). Payment is one responsive component: W (mobile) and WD (desktop) frames both map to it (layout switch at `styles/customer-checkout.css:111` `@media(min-width:1000px)`).
- X2. Default language: Payment.tsx:42 defaults to Thai and stores the choice in `localStorage bitpos.pay.language` (:43). TableMenu.tsx:38 and PayMenu.tsx:13 still default to English and never read that key. All W/WD frames are Thai, so a guest who scans the table QR lands on an English menu, then a Thai pay page.
- X3. Every WD state frame (WD05-WD08, WD11-WD14, WD18-WD20) has the same right card: icon, state title, order id and asset row, "asset change and new payment paused" note, a primary "check status" button and a "ask staff for help" caption. The app renders states as `.checkout-state` (Payment.tsx:216-220) with total, test notice, asset section and fee note. It has NO generic "check status" button (only `checkSaved` for saved payments, :241) and NO "ask staff" affordance anywhere on the pay page. Status refresh is automatic (poll every 5 s, SSE, Payment.tsx:98-100).
- X4. Guest customer ordering has no option picker: product dialog (TableMenu.tsx:148) shows photo, description, price and quantity only; configured options appear only read-only in the bill (`ConfiguredBillLines.tsx:7`).
- X5. No pickup-ready state exists on any guest web page. grep "READY|COLLECTED|pickup" finds nothing in Payment/TableMenu/PayMenu; acknowledgement lives on the shop side and the POSClock device (IncomingOrders.tsx:143-158).
- X6. W frames price in baht (฿95, ฿170) and carry PromptPay/card; WD frames price in USD/USDG devnet. The app shows whatever currency the order has through `formatMoney` (OrderBill.tsx:24, TableMenu.tsx:117) and offers only a Solana wallet.
