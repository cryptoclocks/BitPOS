## Decisions that came from running it on the Android emulator (Pixel_10a, Flutter engine)
- The long public-data disclosure moved behind a "What to know" disclosure (AnimatedSize); the cards were too dense.
- "Cannot be transferred / no monetary value / devnet" is stated once under the list, not on every card.
- The consent switch is gold-on-plum (high contrast); the primary action while a wallet is missing is a full-width gold button.
- Wallet steps are three numbered rows, not one run-on sentence.

## Collectible card art (added the same day)
The badge itself is a collectible card (5:7), not a plain medal: a coffee-cup mascot with a tier accessory on a foil-sheen card. Three editable frames
`A-regulars-card-1/2/3` sit above the User App page (imported from `apps/web/public/loyalty/card-N.svg`); PNG/JSON used by the wallets are in the same folder.
In the app the held badge is a small card with a slow 3D tilt (perspective Y-rotation ±0.25 rad, 4.2 s, reverse) and opens full size on tap (Hero zoom);
with reduced motion the card is static. Art upgrade path (3D/cartoon render): `docs/REGULARS_CARD_ART_PROMPTS_TH.md`.
